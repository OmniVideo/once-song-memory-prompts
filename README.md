# Once Song Memory Prompts

Memory-question worksheets, lyric-detail tips, occasion checklists, and FAQ notes for preparing a personalized song gift on [Once Song](https://oncesong.com/). Once Song turns answers about someone you love — the memories you share and the feeling you want to capture — into a one-of-a-kind song, with their name woven into the chorus.

This repository is maintained by the **Once Song team**. It is worksheets and documentation only. It does not generate audio, call an API, process payments, or replace the live guided questionnaire. Song wording, timing, and unlock options follow the **current site**.

## How to use

1. Pick a worksheet that matches the occasion (birthday, anniversary, wedding, parents, or friends).
2. Answer the prompts offline — short, concrete scenes work better than abstract praise.
3. Open [https://oncesong.com/](https://oncesong.com/), start **Create Your Personalized Song**, and paste or retell the answers in the guided questions.
4. Choose mood, genre, and voice; stay on the page for live production progress; listen privately when ready; unlock the complete song only when the preview feels right.

## Product facts (from the live site)

- Personalized song gift shaped around the person, occasion, memories, and message you share.
- Four steps: share your story → choose the feeling (mood, genre, voice) → hear privately → unlock the complete keepsake.
- You can create and listen for free; no credit card is required to get started. Unlocking the complete song is a one-time purchase shown at checkout (this pack does not list prices).
- Preview of up to about 90 seconds before unlock (Terms). Production progress updates on the same page after submit. Timing is advertised as as fast as about 30 minutes — confirm live.
- Details that feel personal: names (including in the chorus), shared memories, places, favorite qualities, inside jokes, promises, and a heartfelt message.
- Occasions called out on the site include anniversary, birthday, wedding, proposal, holidays, thank-you, celebration, memorial, and more. Christmas edition has its own page and order terms.
- The song identifier / private link is a bearer credential — anyone with the link can open the song page. Keep it private until you are ready to gift.
- Support: support@oncesong.com · Policies: Terms, Privacy, Refund Policy on the site.
