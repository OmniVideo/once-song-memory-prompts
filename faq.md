# FAQ

Answers mirror the Personalized Song Gift FAQs and related policies on [https://oncesong.com/](https://oncesong.com/). Always confirm the live site for wording, timing, and checkout details.

### What is a personalized song gift?

An original song shaped around the person, occasion, memories, and message you share — a one-of-a-kind gift that feels unmistakably theirs.

### Who is it for?

A partner, parent, friend, child, family member, or anyone whose story deserves to be celebrated in a personal way.

### What occasions work well?

Anniversaries, birthdays, weddings, proposals, holidays, thank-you moments, celebrations, and heartfelt memorials (among others on the site).

### What details make it feel personal?

Names, shared memories, meaningful places, favorite qualities, inside jokes, promises, and a message from the heart. The product story highlights the name in the chorus.

### Can I choose music style and voice?

Yes. You choose the mood, genre, and voice that match the person, occasion, and feeling.

### Can I create and listen for free?

Yes. You can create your custom song and listen for free. No credit card is required to get started. Unlocking the complete song is a separate one-time purchase shown at checkout.

### How do I follow progress?

Stay on the page after submitting your story to view live production progress as the song moves through each stage.

### What happens when it is ready?

The page updates when the song is ready to hear. Listen privately, then decide when to unlock the complete song.

### How long does it take?

The homepage advertises “as fast as 30 min.” Treat that as an advertised window and watch the live progress UI for your order.

### Is the song link private?

The song identifier is a bearer access credential. Anyone who knows the identifier or receives the private link can open the song page (and any purchased complete version). Keep the link private.
