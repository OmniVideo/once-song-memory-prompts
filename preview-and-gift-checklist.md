# Preview and gift checklist

Safety and sharing notes from the live Terms, Privacy, and FAQ on [Once Song](https://oncesong.com/).

## While the song is producing

- [ ] Stay on the page to follow live production progress after you submit
- [ ] Keep the tab/session available; use the progress UI rather than guessing status
- [ ] Do not send an unfinished surprise link to the recipient yet

## Private preview

- [ ] Listen yourself first — the product flow is built around hearing privately before the gift moment
- [ ] Check: correct person, name pronunciation, memories not mixed with someone else, tone fits the occasion
- [ ] Terms: preview may be up to about 90 seconds before you unlock the complete song
- [ ] Creative wording can vary; preference alone may not be refund grounds for standard unlocks (see Refund Policy)

## Unlock

- [ ] Review price and email on the checkout page (this pack does not quote prices)
- [ ] Unlocking the complete song is described as a one-time purchase of digital content, not a subscription
- [ ] Confirm you are using the intended purchase email for receipts / support

## Sharing the gift

- [ ] Treat the song identifier / private link as a **bearer credential** — anyone with it can open the song page
- [ ] Share only with people you trust; avoid posting the raw link publicly
- [ ] Standard Terms describe playback through the private link (not a general public-sharing feature). Christmas edition terms mention lyrics + download for personal use — confirm the edition you ordered
- [ ] Keep your email account protected; support contact is support@oncesong.com

## If something goes wrong

Eligible review cases in the Refund Policy include duplicate charges, wrong amount, or inability to access the complete song after payment. Email support@oncesong.com with purchase email, song identifier, transaction reference, and a short description. Christmas satisfaction guarantee (if applicable) is described on the Christmas / Terms pages — follow those live texts.
