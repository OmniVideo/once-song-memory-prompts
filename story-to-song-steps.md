# Story to song — four public steps

Plain-language map of the flow described on [https://oncesong.com/](https://oncesong.com/). Use a worksheet first if you want answers ready before you open the form.

## 1. Share your story

Answer guided questions about:

- the person the song is for
- the occasion
- the moments you want the song to remember

Tip: write 2–5 short scenes (“what happened / where / what it meant”) instead of one long essay. See `choosing-lyric-details.md`.

## 2. Choose the feeling

Select the **mood**, **genre**, and **voice** that fit the person and the message. Sample mood labels on the homepage include Romantic, Emotional, Upbeat, Grateful, and Comforting — use whatever the live form offers when you create the song.

## 3. Hear your song

When the personalized song is ready, listen **privately** and make sure the moment feels right. Stay on the page after submitting to follow live production progress. The Terms describe a preview of up to about 90 seconds before you unlock the complete song.

## 4. Keep the gift

Unlock the complete song and keep a personal keepsake they can replay. Share the private song link only with people you trust (see `preview-and-gift-checklist.md`).

## What this pack does not do

- It does not submit stories or generate audio.
- It does not invent prices, plan names, or guarantee timing — check the live checkout and progress UI.
- Christmas orders use a separate path and terms on [https://oncesong.com/christmas](https://oncesong.com/christmas); confirm that page if you are ordering a Christmas edition.
